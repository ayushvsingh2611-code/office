📖 Ludo Master Rules & Guide

1. Objective

Be the first player to successfully guide all 4 of your tokens from your yard, around the 52-cell outer track, up your home runway, and into the center home triangle.

2. Releasing Tokens

You need to roll a 6 to move a token out of your Yard onto your Starting Square. If all your tokens are in the yard and you don't roll a 6, your turn is forfeited.

3. Bonus Turns & Extra Rolls

You earn an extra dice roll if you:

  • Roll a 6 (max 2 consecutive extra rolls before 3-sixes penalty).
  • Capture an opponent's token.
  • Successfully guide a token into the center home.

4. Safe Squares & Capturing

Starting squares and marked star cells (★) are Safe Squares. Opponents cannot capture your tokens there. On normal squares, landing on an opponent token captures it and sends it back to their yard!

5. The 3 Sixes Penalty

Rolling three 6s in a row instantly forfeits your turn and cancels all accumulated progress for that turn.

6. Exact Home Entry

Tokens require an exact dice roll to step into the final home center. Total distance from yard to home is 56 steps.
