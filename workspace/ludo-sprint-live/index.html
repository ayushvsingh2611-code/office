📜 Rules of Ludo Grand Master

1. Objective

Navigate all 4 of your tokens from your yard, around the 52-tile common track, up your home stretch, and into the center goal.

2. Rolling & 6s

Roll a 6 to bring a token out of your yard onto the start tile. Rolling a 6 grants you an extra bonus roll! However, rolling three 6s in a row forfeits your turn.

3. Captures (Kills)

Landing on a tile occupied by an opponent sends their token back to their yard (+150 pts & bonus roll!). Safe spots (marked with star/shields and start tiles) protect tokens from capture.

4. Scoring Formula

Score = (Goals × 500) + (Kills × 150) + (Steps × 2) + (Yard Exits × 20) + Roll 6 Bonus (+10).
