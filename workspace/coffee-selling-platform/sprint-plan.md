## 🌟 Project Summary: Coffee Selling Platform ("BrewHellas")
- **Core Features:**
  1. **Product Catalog & Filtering:** Browse specialty coffees (Single Origin, Espresso Blends, Cold Brews, Decaf) with roast level filters, tasting notes, and price tags.
  2. **Working Add-to-Cart:** Instant item addition with grind size selection (Whole Bean, Espresso, Drip, French Press), bag size options (250g, 500g, 1kg), and live quantity badges.
  3. **Cart Management Drawer/Page:** Real-time subtotal calculation, tax, shipping estimates, item removal, and quantity adjustments with LocalStorage persistence.
  4. **Seamless Checkout Flow:** Clean multi-field checkout form capturing customer info, delivery address, shipping method, and mock payment method with robust input validation.
  5. **Checkout Details & Order Confirmation:** Comprehensive itemized invoice summary, unique order ID generation, estimated delivery date, and order success state.
