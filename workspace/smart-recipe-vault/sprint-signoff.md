## 🚀 Key Application Features Delivered
- **Recipe Discovery Grid:** Instant search by name, tag, or ingredients with smooth card transitions.
- **Dietary & Cook Time Filters:** Filter instantly by Vegan, Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Keto, and maximum cook time (<15m, <30m, <45m, <60m).
- **Interactive Grocery List Generator:** Automatically aggregates missing ingredients from selected recipes, scales by custom guest servings, and provides interactive checklist management with LocalStorage persistence.
- **Chef Consultation Booking Modal:** Interactive booking system allowing users to select a professional chef, pick a consultation date/slot, specify dietary focus, and receive instant confirmation feedback.
