* **Specialist Assignment:** Shanks (System Architecture & Tech Stack) & Nami (Database Schema & Navigation)

  * Entity-Relationship (ER) Diagram for Patients, Doctors, Appointments, Lab Reports, and ICU Beds.

  * Doctor Desk Dashboard for managing queues, prescriptions, and patient history.
  * Backend REST/GraphQL APIs (Node.js/Python) for authentication, booking, and ICU tracking.
