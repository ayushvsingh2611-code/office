The Ritual Promise

From Volcanic Soil To Your Morning Cup

We partner with regenerative multi-generational coffee farms located on misty volcanic slopes between 1,600 and 2,200 meters above sea level. Slower maturation produces denser beans bursting with intricate florals, vibrant stone fruit acidity, and deep chocolate undertones.

Each batch is drum-roasted on vintage cast-iron equipment, profiling temperature and airflow manually to bring out each micro-lot's truest character.

Barista Handbook

Mastering The Pour

Recommended brew recipes calibrated specifically for our single origin micro-lots.

V60 Pour Over

Best for: Light Roasts & High-Altitude Florals

  • Ratio: 1:16 (18g coffee to 290g water)
  • Water Temp: 93°C / 200°F
  • Grind: Medium-Fine (sea salt)
  • Total Brew Time: 3m 00s

French Press Immersion

Best for: Medium & Dark Roasts with Rich Body

  • Ratio: 1:15 (30g coffee to 450g water)
  • Water Temp: 95°C / 203°F
  • Grind: Coarse (breadcrumbs)
  • Steep Time: 4m 30s

Slow Cold Drip

Best for: Low-acidity chocolate & sweet caramel

  • Ratio: 1:8 (100g coffee to 800g filtered water)
  • Water Temp: Chilled 4°C
  • Grind: Extra Coarse
  • Extraction: 16-18 hours slow steep
