## 1. 📖 User Stories

* **US-01: Product Catalog & Browsing**  
  *As a* coffee enthusiast,  
  *I want to* browse a visually rich catalog of artisan coffee beans with details (origin, roast level, flavor notes, price),  
  *So that* I can discover and select coffees that match my flavor profile preferences.

* **US-02: Category & Attribute Filtering**  
  *As a* shopper,  
  *I want to* filter products by category (e.g., Single Origin, Blends, Decaf, Espresso), roast level (Light, Medium, Dark), and price range,  
  *So that* I can quickly narrow down the selection to what I want to buy without scrolling through the entire catalog.
