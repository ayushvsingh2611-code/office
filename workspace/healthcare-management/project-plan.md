* **Specialist Assignment:** Luffy (Orchestrator)

* **Lead:** Hancock (Requirements & User Experience Specialist)

* **Lead:** Shanks (System Architect) & Nami (Data Navigator)

* **Specialist Assignment:** Franky (Core UI & Modals) & Usopp (Responsive Styling & Search/Filter Components)

### **Milestone 7: Review & Sign-Off (The Ultimate Victory Feast)**
